Job Market Paper

Outcomes of Property Tax Regressivity

Tax assessments are regressive with respect to house price, leaving owners of cheaper homes with higher effective tax rates. Using Mecklenburg County’s seven-year reassessment cycle, I find that bottom-quartile homes sell at an 18 percent discount and see 64 percent more foreclosure filings in the two to three years after a reassessment.
